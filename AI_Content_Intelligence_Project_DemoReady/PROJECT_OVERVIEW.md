
# Project23-oriented portfolio project

## Problem
Content teams often have ideas and production activity but lack one operating layer connecting strategy, execution and performance learning.

## Solution
The AI Content Intelligence platform creates a closed loop:

1. Define campaign objective.
2. Generate content strategy.
3. Prioritize ideas by impact and effort.
4. Track production.
5. Capture performance.
6. Analyze what worked.
7. Generate experiments for the next cycle.

## Management angle
The project is intentionally not only an AI generator. It demonstrates:
- prioritization
- ownership
- deadlines
- workflow stages
- decision support
- KPI tracking
- continuous improvement

## AI angle
The LLM is used for:
- strategy generation
- content ideation
- structured campaign planning
- performance interpretation
- experiment recommendations

## Interview pitch
"I built this because I was interested in the management side of AI-led content. Instead of making another content generator, I wanted to model how AI could support the whole operating cycle—from planning and prioritization to production tracking, performance analysis and deciding what the team should test next."
